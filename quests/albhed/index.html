Al Bhed Cheat Sheet

During the Temple Password quest, a password flashes on stream in Al Bhed for about a second. Match the symbols to their letters below, then type !password and the word in chat.

Your word appears here, the way it flashes on stream.

The Al Bhed alphabet

Each symbol stands for one letter. Tap a symbol to add its letter to the box above, so you can spell out a password from the symbols you saw.

    Passwords are Final Fantasy names: characters, spells, items, monsters and places. Capital letters are all you need.
