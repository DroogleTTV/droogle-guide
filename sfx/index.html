SFX commands

Every sound effect you can fire in Droogle’s chat. Press play to hear one, and tap a command to copy it.

SFX commands are for subscribers (and mods) unless marked otherwise. Each one has its own cooldown. Use them to punctuate a moment: misuse can cost you SFX access. Random commands play one of many clips, so the preview here is a sample.
